mail-agent

mail-agent is a private, single-user tool that connects one person's own Gmail account to a locally run AI assistant. It does not offer sign-ups and is not a public service.

What it does

  • Reads and searches the owner's own Gmail messages on request.
  • Summarizes threads and extracts action items and deadlines.
  • Creates draft replies for the owner to review; nothing is sent automatically.
  • Applies labels to help organize the owner's mailbox.

All processing happens on the owner's own machine. The owner authorizes access through Google's standard OAuth consent screen, and access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
