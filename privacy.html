Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how mail-agent ("the App") handles Google user data. The App is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner and is used only for the owner's own Google Account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google data the App accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth consent screen, the App requests access to the owner's Gmail account in order to read, search, label, and create draft messages on the owner's behalf. The App does not access data for any account other than the one the owner authorizes.

How Google data is used

  • To display, search, and summarize the owner's own email.
  • To extract action items and deadlines from the owner's email.
  • To create draft replies and apply labels requested by the owner.

Google user data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

The App runs locally on the owner's own device. OAuth credentials are stored locally on that device. Email content is processed locally and is not transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server operated by the developer. The developer operates no server that receives Google user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with any third party, and is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide the features requested by the owner or as required by law.

Limited Use disclosure

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the App's access to their Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further access. Locally stored credentials and any cached data can be deleted by removing the App's local credential files from the owner's device.
